Curio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026 · 最后更新：2026 年 8 月 15 日

1. Introduction

Welcome to Curio. Curio is an iOS app for recording, organizing, and revisiting your personal item collections. We care about your privacy and aim to keep your collection data on your own device and within Apple system services.

By using this app, you agree to Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

2. Information We Do Not Actively Collect

Curio currently does not operate its own server and does not actively collect, sell, or share the following information:

  • Your contacts, address book, or precise location
  • Microphone recordings
  • Live camera feed or your full photo library
  • Advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking data, or browsing history
  • Data generated by third-party advertising, analytics, or attribution SDKs

3. Data Stored in the App

To provide collection-recording features, Curio stores the data you create or import, including:

  • Item names, categories, prices, purchase dates, notes, and preference ratings
  • Series names, series notes, and item-series relationships
  • Timeline events, reminder rules, and app preferences
  • Photos you choose to take or import as item cover images

By default, this data is stored in the app's local sandbox and is used for display, editing, and review inside Curio. If you explicitly enable iCloud Sync, the data described in Section 7 is also stored in your private iCloud database.

Curio Plus also provides full-archive export and import. An exported .curiobackup package contains your items, images, series, timeline data, reminders, and their relationships, and is not password-encrypted. You choose where to save the package through the iOS file interface; Curio does not upload it to a Curio-owned server. Import validates the package and, only after your confirmation, replaces the current archive. Curio keeps only the latest local pre-import recovery point.

4. Photos and Image Processing

Curio uses camera and photo access to help you create item covers. Cutout, outline, and cover-generation processing happen locally on your device. We do not upload your photos to Curio-owned servers.

When you choose an image from your photo library, Curio only accesses the image you explicitly select and does not read your full photo library.

5. System Permissions

Curio may request the following system permissions:

  • Camera: to take item photos.
  • Photos: to select images from your photo library and, when you choose to save, write Curio-generated images or group or contact QR codes to Photos.
  • Notifications: to deliver item reminder rules.
  • Face ID / device authentication: to provide app lock. Biometric data is handled by iOS, and Curio does not receive or store your Face ID data.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Curio Plus purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple StoreKit and the App Store. Curio does not receive your card number, payment account password, or complete payment details. The app only receives transaction status returned by Apple to determine whether Curio Plus features are available.

7. iCloud and Third-Party Services

Curio supports optional iCloud Sync as a Curio Plus feature. Sync is off by default. When you explicitly enable it while signed in to iCloud, Curio uploads your full private archive—including item, series, timeline, reminder, and image data—to the private CloudKit database associated with your iCloud account and keeps that archive in sync across devices using the same account. Changes and deletions can propagate to those devices. Sync is not a backup.

Apple operates iCloud and CloudKit. Data in the private database is available only to your iCloud account by default, counts toward your iCloud storage, and is governed by Apple's privacy policy and security mechanisms. Curio does not upload this archive to a Curio-owned server.

Except for Apple system capabilities such as StoreKit, iCloud, notifications, Face ID, and Safari View, Curio currently does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services.

8. Data Retention, Deletion, and Control

  • Local data remains on your device until you delete records inside Curio or uninstall the app. Uninstalling usually removes local app data from that device.
  • You can delete items, series, or events inside Curio. If iCloud Sync is on, keep it on until the app reports that synchronization is complete so those deletions can propagate to your other synced devices and private iCloud database.
  • Turning off iCloud Sync in Curio stops new transfers but retains existing archives on the device and in iCloud. Uninstalling Curio does not by itself delete data already stored in iCloud.
  • You can manage whether Curio may use iCloud in your Apple Account's iCloud settings. If you need help understanding or deleting Curio data, contact us using the addresses below. Because the archive is stored in your private iCloud database, Curio does not have direct access to inspect it or delete it on your behalf.

9. Children's Privacy

Curio is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe we accidentally processed information related to a child, please contact us.

2. 我们不主动收集的信息

Curio 目前不运营自有服务器，也不会主动收集、出售或共享以下信息：

  • 您的联系人、通讯录或地理位置
  • 麦克风录音
  • 相机实时画面或照片库完整内容
  • 广告标识符、跨 App 追踪数据或浏览记录
  • 第三方广告、分析或归因 SDK 产生的数据

3. 应用中保存的数据

为了提供收藏记录功能，Curio 会在您的设备本地保存您主动创建或导入的数据，包括：

  • 器物名称、分类、价格、购入日期、备注、喜好程度等资料
  • 系列名称、系列备注和器物归属关系
  • 生命线事件、提醒规则和应用偏好设置
  • 您选择拍摄或导入并用于器物封面的图片

默认情况下，这些数据保存在 App 的本地沙盒中，用于在应用内展示、编辑和回看。如果您主动开启 iCloud 同步，第 7 节所述数据也会保存在您的私有 iCloud 数据库中。

Curio Plus 还提供完整档案导出与导入。导出的 .curiobackup 文档包包含器物、图片、系列、生命线、提醒及其关系，且不提供密码加密。您通过 iOS 文件界面自行选择保存位置，Curio 不会把它上传到 Curio 自有服务器。导入会先校验文档包，并仅在您确认后完整替换当前档案；Curio 只保留最近一次导入前的本地恢复点。

4. 图片与识别处理

Curio 的相机和相册能力用于帮助您创建器物封面。图片抠图、描边和封面生成在设备本地处理。我们不会把您的照片上传到 Curio 自有服务器。

当您从照片库选择图片时，Curio 只会访问您明确选择的图片，不会读取整个照片库。

5. 系统权限

Curio 可能会请求以下系统权限：

  • 相机权限：用于拍摄器物照片。
  • 照片权限：用于从照片库选择图片导入，以及在您主动保存时把 Curio 生成的图片、用户群二维码或联系人二维码写入照片库。
  • 通知权限：用于器物提醒规则。
  • Face ID / 设备认证：用于应用锁。生物识别信息由 iOS 系统处理，Curio 不会获取或保存您的 Face ID 数据。

6. 购买与订阅

Curio Plus 的购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 与 App Store 处理。Curio 不会获取您的银行卡号、支付账户密码或完整支付资料。应用只会接收 Apple 返回的交易状态，用于判断 Curio Plus 功能是否可用。

7. iCloud 与第三方服务

Curio Plus 提供可选的 iCloud 同步，默认关闭。当您已登录 iCloud 并主动开启同步时，Curio 会把完整私人档案（包括器物、系列、生命线、提醒和图片数据）上传到与您 iCloud 账户关联的私有 CloudKit 数据库，并在使用同一账户的设备之间保持同步。修改和删除可能传播到这些设备；同步不是备份。

iCloud 与 CloudKit 由 Apple 运营。私有数据库中的数据默认仅您的 iCloud 账户可以访问，会占用您的 iCloud 存储空间，并受 Apple 的隐私政策与安全机制约束。Curio 不会把这份档案上传到 Curio 自有服务器。

除 Apple 提供的系统能力（如 StoreKit、iCloud、通知、Face ID、Safari 视图）外，Curio 当前不集成第三方广告或分析服务。

8. 数据保留、删除与控制

  • 本地数据会保留在设备上，直至您在 Curio 内删除相关记录或卸载 App。卸载通常会删除该设备上的本地应用数据。
  • 您可以在 Curio 内删除器物、系列或事件。如果 iCloud 同步已开启，请保持同步开启，直至 App 显示同步完成，以便删除传播到其他同步设备和您的私有 iCloud 数据库。
  • 在 Curio 内关闭 iCloud 同步会停止传输新修改，但会保留设备和 iCloud 中已有的档案。卸载 Curio 本身也不会删除已存入 iCloud 的数据。
  • 您可以在 Apple 账户的 iCloud 设置中管理 Curio 是否可使用 iCloud。如需了解或删除 Curio 数据方面的帮助，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。由于档案保存在您的私有 iCloud 数据库中，Curio 无法直接查看，也无法代您删除。
