Curio / 器物册

隐私政策

这份政策说明 Curio 当前版本如何处理你主动创建的器物档案、图片、提醒与可选的私有 iCloud 同步。

最后更新：2026 年 8 月 25 日 · Last updated: August 25, 2026

简体中文

1. 适用范围

Curio / 器物册是一款用于建立、整理和回看个人器物档案的 iOS 与 iPadOS 应用。本政策适用于 Curio 当前版本及这两个页面所提供的支持入口。

2. 你创建的数据与本地存储

为了让你建立和使用器物档案，Curio 会在设备本地保存你主动输入、导入或生成的数据，例如器物和系列的名称、分类、存放位置、日期、价格、备注、生命线事件、提醒规则、偏好设置，以及你选择拍摄或导入的封面图片与贴纸素材。数据用于应用内的展示、编辑、搜索、提醒、备份和恢复。

Curio 不运营用于收集这些档案内容的自有服务器。默认情况下，档案保留在设备的应用数据空间中。

3. 相机、照片与图片处理

只有在你发起拍摄、从照片库选择图片或保存生成图片时，Curio 才请求相应的系统权限。通过相册选择时，Curio 只使用你明确选择的图片，不会主动浏览整个照片库。

自动抠图、描边和封面生成在设备本地进行；Curio 不会把你的照片上传到 Curio 自有服务器。

4. 可选的私有 iCloud 同步

只有在你主动开启 iCloud 同步且当前版本允许使用该功能时，Curio 才会通过 Apple 的私有 CloudKit 数据库（容器标识符为 iCloud.com.diwu.Curio）同步相应的档案数据和关联图片，以便在你的设备间保持一致。该数据由 Apple 的 iCloud 服务处理，并适用 Apple 的隐私条款。

关闭同步会让 Curio 停止以云端模式连接该档案；这不会承诺立即删除已经存在于 iCloud 的副本。你可通过 Apple ID 的 iCloud 设置管理相关云端数据；如需使用帮助，也可联系本页所列支持邮箱。

5. 提醒与设备认证

如果你允许通知，Curio 会使用 iOS 本地通知安排器物提醒；提醒的标题和时间可能显示在你的设备通知中。Curio 不运营推送通知服务器来发送这些提醒。

如果你开启 App Lock，Curio 会请求 Face ID、Touch ID 或设备密码完成认证。生物特征由操作系统处理；Curio 只接收认证是否成功的结果，不会读取或保存你的生物特征模板。

6. 购买与第三方服务

Curio Plus 的购买、恢复购买和交易验证由 Apple 的 StoreKit 与 App Store 处理。Curio 不会获得或保存你的支付卡号、支付账户密码或完整支付明细；应用仅使用 Apple 返回的交易与权益状态来决定相应功能是否可用。

除 Apple 提供的 iCloud、StoreKit、系统通知、设备认证及应用内打开链接所使用的系统服务外，Curio 当前不集成广告、分析、归因或跨 App 追踪 SDK，也不会出售你的档案数据。

7. 数据控制、删除与保留

你可以在 Curio 内删除自己创建的器物、系列、事件和提醒；删除后，Curio 不再将这些记录用于应用内功能。你导出的备份文件由你自行选择保存位置和管理；如不再需要，请在相应位置自行删除。

本机数据、导出的备份与 iCloud 中已有副本可能分别受设备、文件系统和 Apple iCloud 的保留机制影响。Curio 不维护可供我们代为检索或删除你的器物档案的自有账户数据库。

8. 政策更新与联系

当 Curio 的数据处理方式发生变化时，我们会更新本页的内容和“最后更新”日期。对于隐私问题、数据控制帮助或一般支持，请发送邮件至 wulingxi93@qq.com。

使用 Curio 同时适用 Apple 的 标准最终用户许可协议。

English

1. Scope

Curio is an iOS and iPadOS app for creating, organizing, and revisiting personal item records. This policy applies to the current version of Curio and to the support entry provided by these pages.

2. Data you create and local storage

To create and use item records, Curio stores data that you choose to enter, import, or generate on your device. This can include item and series names, categories, storage locations, dates, prices, notes, life-line events, reminder rules, preferences, and the cover photos or sticker materials you choose to create or import. Curio uses this data for in-app display, editing, search, reminders, backup, and restore.

Curio doesn't operate its own server to collect this archive content. By default, the archive stays in the app's data space on your device.

3. Camera, photos, and image processing

Curio requests the relevant system permission only when you choose to take a photo, select one from your library, or save an image that you generate. When you use the photo picker, Curio uses only the image you explicitly select and doesn't proactively browse your entire photo library.

Automatic cutout, outlining, and cover generation run on your device. Curio doesn't upload your photos to Curio-operated servers.

4. Optional private iCloud sync

Only when you explicitly enable iCloud sync and the feature is available in your version does Curio use Apple's private CloudKit database, with the container identifier iCloud.com.diwu.Curio, to synchronize the relevant archive data and associated images between your devices. Apple processes that data through iCloud, subject to Apple's privacy terms.

Turning sync off makes Curio stop connecting to that archive in cloud mode; it doesn't promise immediate deletion of a copy that already exists in iCloud. You can manage related cloud data in your Apple ID iCloud settings, or contact the support email on this page for help.

5. Reminders and device authentication

If you allow notifications, Curio uses iOS local notifications to schedule item reminders. A reminder's title and time can appear in notifications on your device. Curio doesn't operate a push-notification server for these reminders.

If you enable App Lock, Curio asks Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode to authenticate you. The operating system handles biometric information; Curio receives only whether authentication succeeded and doesn't read or store a biometric template.

6. Purchases and third-party services

Curio Plus purchases, purchase restoration, and transaction verification are handled by Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. Curio doesn't receive or store your payment-card number, payment-account password, or complete payment details. The app uses the transaction and entitlement status that Apple returns only to determine whether the related features are available.

Other than Apple services for iCloud, StoreKit, system notifications, device authentication, and the system services used when opening links in the app, Curio currently doesn't integrate advertising, analytics, attribution, or cross-app tracking SDKs, and doesn't sell your archive data.

7. Control, deletion, and retention

You can delete items, series, events, and reminders that you create in Curio. After deletion, Curio no longer uses those records for in-app features. You choose where to save exported backup files and manage them yourself; delete an export from that location when you no longer need it.

Local data, exported backups, and existing iCloud copies can each be subject to the retention mechanisms of your device, file system, and Apple iCloud. Curio doesn't maintain its own account database from which we can retrieve or delete your item archive for you.

8. Policy changes and contact

When Curio's data practices change, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date. For privacy questions, help with data controls, or general support, email wulingxi93@qq.com.

Your use of Curio is also subject to Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.
