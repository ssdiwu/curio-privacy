English

Need help?

For support, feedback, or feature requests, email:

wulingxi93@qq.com

To help us investigate, please include when relevant:

  • your Curio version, iOS/iPadOS version, and device model;
  • the steps before the issue and the result you expected; and
  • a screenshot or recording that doesn't expose collection data you prefer to keep private.

Curio doesn't offer phone support. Please don't email passwords, payment-card details, or private archives you don't want to share.
